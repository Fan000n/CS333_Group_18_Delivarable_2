Question: Which community has the largest number of sales of units and villas, and how many sales is it? Is this visualization effective in finding this community? Why?

AL BARSHA SOUTH FOURTH (community 681) has the most unit and villa sales, with 3,925 sales.

Not very effective. The colour of each community encodes the median price per m², not the number of sales, so NO its not visible on the map at all. The only way to find it is to hover over communities one by one and compare their tooltips, and there are 226 communities, many of them tiny or in the desert. But to make this easier, we added a toggleable option that overlays circles on the communities, with each circle's size showing the number of sales.

Design choices for reliable colours:
  • Communities with fewer sales than the chosen minimum (default 10) are not coloured, because a median of a handful of transactions is not trustworthy. They are drawn in dark grey and still show their count and price in the tooltip. Communities with no sales at all are light grey, so all 226 communities remain visible.
